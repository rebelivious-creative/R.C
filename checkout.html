LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

1. Payment & Commencement

Full payment is required prior to the commencement of any creative production. Due to the custom nature of our architecture, payments are non-refundable once production has begun.

2. Revisions & Scope

Projects include the specified rounds of revisions. A "revision" constitutes minor adjustments to the presented concept. Deviations from the original creative brief will be quoted as a separate project.

3. Intellectual Property

Upon final payment, the client is granted commercial rights to use the finalized visual assets. Rebelivious Creative retains the right to display completed work within our visual archive. Raw source files remain the property of Rebelivious Creative unless a separate license is acquired.

4. Timelines

Project delivery depends on timely feedback. Projects delayed by the client for more than 30 days will be considered paused, and a reactivation fee may apply.
